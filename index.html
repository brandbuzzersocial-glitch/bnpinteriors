WHO WE ARE

India's Premier Turnkey Contracting & Interior Studio

BNP Interiors was founded in 2006 by visionary entrepreneur Mr. Punamchand Kularia. In just over 18 years, the company has carved a niche in high-end interiors for the hospitality, commercial, and private sectors — built on an unwavering commitment to integrity and transparency. Today, under the leadership of Punam D. Kularia, Founder & Managing Director, BNP Interiors is one of India's leading civil and interior turnkey contracting firms, with over 60% of its business coming from repeat clients.

OUR METHODOLOGY

A Seamless Path From Concept To Completion

Our 4-stage turnkey process ensures complete transparency and adherence to timelines through our in-house ERP system.

01

Consultation & Site Assessment

We analyze brief requirements, site conditions, technical specifications, and budgetary boundaries.

02

Concept, Design & Visualization

Developing 2D drawings, 3D photorealistic visual models, BIM coordination, and material selection.

03

Procurement & Production

In-house HOMAG manufacturing run in parallel with site civil & MEP works tracked through ERP.

04

Execution & Handover

On-site installation, stringent quality control audits, final walkthrough, and post-handover support.
