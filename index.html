MILESTONES OF EXCELLENCE

Our Journey Through The Years

From our founding in 2006 to delivering 700+ turnkey civil and interior landmarks across India, explore the defining milestones of BNP Interiors.

2006

The Inception & First Landmark Win

Founded by Mr. Punamchand Kularia, BNP Interiors launched with a landmark ₹9 crore contract for Shoppers Stop, establishing our core values of transparency and engineering precision.

2010

Expansion into Commercial & Banking Fit-Outs

Expanded pan-India operations into corporate headquarters and financial banking floors for prestigious clients including HDFC Bank, Citibank, and BNP Paribas.

2015

In-House Precision Factory Launch

Established our state-of-the-art Navi Mumbai manufacturing facility equipped with German (HOMAG) and Italian automated machinery for factory-finished precision furniture.

2020

Luxury Hospitality Dominance

Scaled turnkey civil and interior fit-out execution across luxury hotel brands including Taj, Marriott, Hyatt, Radisson, Accor, and IHG across India.

2026

700+ Landmark Executions & 35M+ Sq. Ft.

Under the leadership of Mr. Punam D. Kularia, BNP Interiors reaches 700+ completed projects, 35M+ sq. ft. transformed, and a 5,000+ strong workforce with over 60% repeat clients.

OUR METHODOLOGY

A Seamless Path From Concept To Completion

Our 4-stage turnkey process ensures complete transparency and adherence to timelines through our in-house ERP system.

01

Consultation & Site Assessment

We analyze brief requirements, site conditions, technical specifications, and budgetary boundaries.

02

Concept, Design & Visualization

Developing 2D drawings, 3D photorealistic visual models, BIM coordination, and material selection.

03

Procurement & Production

In-house HOMAG manufacturing run in parallel with site civil & MEP works tracked through ERP.

04

Execution & Handover

On-site installation, stringent quality control audits, final walkthrough, and post-handover support.
