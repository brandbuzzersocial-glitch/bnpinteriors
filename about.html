ABOUT OUR STUDIO

20 Years of Mastering Luxury With Vision and Precision

Founded in 2006 by Mr. Punamchand Kularia, BNP Interiors began its journey with a ₹9 crore landmark win from Shoppers Stop. What started as a modest venture has grown into one of India's leading civil and interior turnkey contracting firms — delivering over 700 projects across hospitality, commercial, banking, IT, and residential sectors.
