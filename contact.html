FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a typical project take?

Timeline varies by project scope. A single room redesign can take 2–4 weeks, while full home renovations typically span 3–6 months. We'll give you a clear timeline during our initial consultation.

What is your design process?

We follow a four-step process: Initial Consultation → Design & Planning → Implementation → Project Handover. You're involved at every stage to ensure the final result exceeds your expectations.

Do you work with all budgets?

Yes! We offer services ranging from budget-conscious consultations to full luxury renovations. During our initial call, we'll discuss your budget openly and tailor our approach accordingly.

Can you work with my existing furniture?

Absolutely! We're skilled at incorporating your existing pieces into new designs. We'll assess what works, what can be repurposed, and what might need to be replaced to achieve your vision.
